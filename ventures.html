Beyond our core advisory and engineering services, 20squares incubates specialized entities to address fundamental challenges in cryptography and systems theory.

Cryptography

Neverlocal

Neverlocal is our dedicated cryptography engineering lab. We build production-grade implementations of post-quantum primitives and zero-knowledge proof systems. Our focus is on practical performance and side-channel resistance for real-world applications.

We provide the cryptographic infrastructure that secures high-value networks against both classical and quantum threats.

Applied Research

Cybercat Institute

The Institute for Categorical Cybernetics (Cybercat) is our non-profit research initiative. It bridges the gap between theoretical computer science and systems engineering.

While we maintain academic rigor, the Institute's mandate is to translate advanced mathematical concepts into actionable engineering frameworks for complex adaptive systems.
