Engineering Excellence

We are a team of engineers, mathematicians, and economists dedicated to optimizing complex systems. We combine deep knowledge with rigorous industry experience across Crypto, Fintech, Energy, and Gaming.

Our Philosophy

We believe that high-stakes systems—whether a BESS plant, an airline pricing engine, or a DeFi protocol—require more than just code; they require a fundamental understanding of incentives and dynamics. We approach every project with an engineering mindset, prioritizing robustness, correctness, and efficiency.

Our History

20squares started as a specialized research group initially focused on the Ethereum blockchain. Since then we have evolved into a full-stack engineering firm. Theoretical designs are only valuable if they can be securely implemented in production. Today, we can handle the entire lifecycle of a project.
