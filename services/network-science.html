Network Science

Map the invisible. We use advanced graph analytics to uncover hidden structures, centralization risks, and improved pathways for growth.

The Challenge

Data is not just a list of items; it's a web of connections. In financial markets, social networks, and supply chains, the relationships between entities matter more than the entities themselves. Ignoring these topologies leaves you blind to systemic risks and viral opportunities.

Our Methodology

We treat your data as a graph. By applying algorithms from network science—centrality measures, community detection, and flow analysis—we reveal the underlying architecture of your system. This allows us to spot bottlenecks, measure true decentralization, and predict contagion.

Applications

  • Risk Concentration: Moving beyond simple metrics to measure true structural dependencies and single points of failure.
  • Contagion Modeling: Predicting how a default or failure cascades through the counterparty network.
  • Customer Segmentation: Clustering users based on interaction patterns for targeted marketing and retention.
