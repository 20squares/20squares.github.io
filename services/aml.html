Service Vertical 05

AML & Fraud Detection

Safeguard your platform against illicit activity with automated, real-time monitoring and compliance solutions.

The Challenge

Regulatory requirements are tightening globally. Manual compliance is slow, costly, and prone to error. You need precise, automated tools to detect money laundering and fraud without stifling legitimate user activity or compromising user experience.

Our Methodology

We deploy advanced pattern recognition algorithms and integrate with leading sanction lists. Our systems monitor transaction flows in real-time, utilizing both on-chain heuristics and off-chain intelligence to flag suspicious behavior while minimizing false positives. Crucially, we employ explainable AI to ensure all automated decisions are transparent and fully auditable by regulators.

Implementation Pipeline

End-to-End Execution

Data Aggregation

Unifying on-chain and off-chain data sources for a complete, 360-degree view of activity.

Behavioral Analysis

Machine learning models to detect structuring, layering, and other complex AML topologies, backed by explainable AI for clear audit trails.

Real-time Alerting

Instant notifications for high-risk transactions and sanctioned entity interactions.

Case Management

Streamlined workflows for deep investigation and regulatory reporting (SARs).

Applications

  • Transaction Monitoring: Real-time screening of deposits, withdrawals, and transfers.
  • Sanctions Screening: Automated checks against OFAC, EU, UN, and other global watchlists.
  • Account Profiling: Risk scoring based on historical interactions and funds provenance.
