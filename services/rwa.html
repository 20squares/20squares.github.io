RWA Tokenization

Bridging the gap between traditional finance and distributed ledger technology through robust legal-technical frameworks.

The Challenge

The tokenization of real-world assets (RWA) presents a multi-faceted optimization problem. It requires the simultaneous satisfaction of legal compliance constraints, technical security requirements, and economic viability conditions. Naive implementations often fail due to a lack of coherence between the on-chain representation and the off-chain legal reality.

Our Methodology

We employ a "full pipeline" approach to RWA engineering. Instead of merely implementing smart contracts, we architect the entire system. This includes the design of the legal wrapper (SPVs, Trusts), the specification of the token model and its economics, and the formal verification of the settlement logic.
