RWA Tokenization

Bridging the gap between traditional finance and distributed ledger technology through robust legal-technical frameworks. Our focus lies on tokenization of debt. We have worked on several projects including token based alternatives to factorization; private credit tokenization design.

The Challenge

The tokenization of real-world assets (RWA) presents a multi-faceted optimization problem. It requires the simultaneous satisfaction of legal compliance constraints, technical security requirements, and economic viability conditions. Naive implementations often fail due to a lack of coherence between the on-chain representation and the off-chain legal reality.

Our Methodology

We employ a "full pipeline" approach to RWA engineering. We do not merely implement smart contracts; we architect the entire system. This includes the design of the legal wrapper (SPVs, Trusts), the specification of the token model, and the formal verification of the settlement logic.
