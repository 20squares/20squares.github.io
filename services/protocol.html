The Challenge

DeFi protocols are high-value targets. A single vulnerability in code or economic logic can lead to total loss of funds. Building a successful protocol requires a deep understanding of both software security and market dynamics.

Our Methodology

We operate at the lowest levels of the stack. From core Ethereum protocol details like inflation control mechanisms to secure smart contract architecture, we build systems that are robust by design. We don't just advise; we implement, test, and deploy.
